Finding a flatmate should not feel like a gamble. Most of us scroll through endless listings, message strangers and hope for the best. 360 FlatMates matches you with flatmates who fit your lifestyle, not just your budget.

COMPATIBILITY MATCHING

Every profile shows a compatibility score across seven areas: sleep schedule, cleanliness, food, smoking, drinking, guests, and work style. You see the score before you send a message.

FLATS AND FLATMATES, YOUR WAY

Looking for a room, listing a spare one, or both? Pick your mode and the app adapts. Room posters get a Post tab. Flat seekers get an Explore tab with a map.

DISCOVER AND EXPLORE

Browse listings for your city, budget and preferences. Search by area or landmark. Filter by budget, room type, furnishing, gender preference and move-in date. Every listing shows rent, deposit, maintenance, amenities and photos.

SWIPE TO MATCH

Swipe right to like, left to pass. When both people like each other, it is a match. Start with an icebreaker or answer three quick questions. The compatibility breakdown stays visible, so the first chat starts with something to talk about.

CHAT WITH CONTEXT

Messages stay inside the app, so you do not share your phone number until you are ready. Send photos, see read receipts and use icebreaker prompts. Each conversation shows the listing you are talking about.

SCHEDULE VISITS

Found a flat you like? Request a visit from the listing or the chat. Pick a date and a time slot, add a note, and track your visits in one place.

LIST YOUR ROOM IN MINUTES

Got a spare room? The step-by-step builder takes you through room details, amenities, photos, rent, deposit and maintenance. Describe your day and the flatmate you want. Listings are reviewed before they go live.

SAFETY AND TRUST

Phone numbers are verified when you add them. Block or report anyone at any time. You can hide your last name or your exact location. Visit the room before you pay, and keep the conversation in the app until you trust the person.

BUILT FOR INDIA

Available in English and Hindi. Works across Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai and Mumbai, and more cities are coming. Light, dark and system themes.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Sign up with your phone, email, Google or Apple
2. Say what you are looking for
3. Take the lifestyle quiz
4. Set your budget and move-in date
5. Discover, swipe, match and chat
6. Schedule a visit and find your home
